Hermes Gmail and Calendar

Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy describes the current Hermes Gmail and Calendar integration within Published Project 1. It is a personal automation integration operated by its owner.

Google data accessed and used

With the owner's authorization, the integration accesses basic Google account identity information, Gmail, and Google Calendar. Account identity identifies the connected account.

  • Gmail: message and thread content, attachments, metadata, labels, and drafts needed for requested email workflows. Authorized features can read and search messages, compose drafts, send messages, and manage labels.
  • Google Calendar: calendars, event details, and availability needed for requested scheduling workflows. Authorized features can view and manage calendars and events.

Data is used to perform the owner's requested tasks and return results. Available actions depend on Google permissions and the controls enabled for the requesting workflow. This website itself does not connect to Google accounts or display private Google data.

Processing and sharing

The integration communicates with Google APIs. When the owner uses a selected AI assistant or tool service, relevant requests, results, and Google data may be sent to that service for processing. Email or event content included in a task may therefore be processed outside the owner's self-hosted infrastructure.

Each selected service's privacy policy and account settings govern its processing, retention, and any use of data for model training. This policy does not promise training exclusions or retention limits for those services.

Credentials and stored data

Google OAuth credentials, including tokens needed to maintain authorized access, are stored on the owner's controlled, self-hosted infrastructure. They are not published in this website or its public source repository.

Task results, histories, cached data, or operational records may also exist in the owner's systems or selected services, depending on how the workflows are configured. There is no single fixed retention period promised across those systems.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can stop the integration and revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not automatically erase data already held by other systems.

The owner controls removal of stored credentials and any local records. Data held by selected AI or tool services must be managed through those services' controls or support channels. Deleting a local record does not delete the original Gmail message or Google Calendar event; those remain subject to Google's account controls.

This public information site

These pages contain no scripts, analytics, forms, or account-registration features. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions and changes

For privacy or deletion assistance, use the support contact shown on the app's Google authorization screen. This policy will be updated when the integration's data practices change.
