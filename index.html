Hermes Gmail and Calendar

Hermes Gmail and Calendar is the Google-connected integration within this project. It connects the owner's authorized Google account to personal automation and user-selected AI and tool services.

Its current features support finding and reading Gmail messages, working with drafts and messages, organizing labels, and viewing and managing Google calendars and events. Actions depend on the permissions the owner grants and the controls enabled for each workflow.

The integration is operated for personal use. This public site provides information about the app; it does not offer account registration or collect email or calendar data.

Access stays under the owner's control

Google access is granted through Google's authorization screen. The owner can limit permitted workflows, stop using the integration, or revoke its access in Google Account connections.

The privacy policy explains what Google data the integration accesses, where credentials are stored, and how selected AI and tool services may process data.

Questions

Use the support contact shown on the app's Google authorization screen for questions about access, privacy, or deletion.
